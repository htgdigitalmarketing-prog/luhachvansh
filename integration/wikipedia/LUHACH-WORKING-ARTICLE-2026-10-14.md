## Luhach

**Luhach** (Hindi: **लुहाच**) is a name used for a Jat *gotra*, or clan, in northern India, and as a surname. The community's website identifies Luhach families in Haryana, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh.[1] The surname also appears in regional newspaper coverage.[3]

### Genealogical documentation

Hindi Book Centre catalogues a Hindi book titled *Luhach Gotra Ka Itihas*, by Karmbir Singh Luhach, with a publication year of 2024 and ISBN 9788194327080.[2]

The Luhach family website separately carries a historical and genealogical account attributed to Colonel Karambir Singh Luhach and dated 21 May 2025. That account describes the collection of family genealogies from village residents and hereditary genealogists' registers. It also states that written evidence for the community's early history before 860 CE was unavailable to its author.[4]

### Use of the name

In September 2024, *Amar Ujala* referred to the Indian para-badminton player Nitesh Kumar as Nitesh Luhach in its coverage of his Paralympic gold medal, associating him with Nandha village in Charkhi Dadri, Haryana.[3] This report documents use of the name; it does not, by itself, establish his genealogical relationship to other Luhach families.
